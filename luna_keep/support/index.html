Luna Keep Support

Questions or problems? Email thecodingwombats@gmail.com. Please do not send health data by email.

Common questions

The app shows no data from Health. Open Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Luna Keep and turn on the types you want to share. Luna Keep also shows Health access in its own Settings.

Do I need an Apple Watch? No. You can type basal temperature from a thermometer and log your period, LH tests and mucus by hand.

How do I move to a new iPhone? Turn on backups in Luna Keep's Settings and save to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new iPhone, install Luna Keep and restore the file with your passphrase. We cannot recover a lost passphrase.

Can I use Luna Keep for contraception? No. Its estimates are for information only, and pregnancy is possible on low days.

Does Luna Keep work without Apple Intelligence? Yes. All cycle rules, temperature charts, LH strip logging, PDF exports and encrypted backups work on any iPhone running iOS 26. When Apple Intelligence isn't available, Luna Keep uses built-in on-device text templates for daily summaries and cycle reviews.

Are my LH test strip photos uploaded anywhere? No. Strip photos are aligned and analysed on your iPhone and stored privately inside the app, not in your Photos library and never on a server. They are included only if you turn on passphrase-encrypted backups.

Is there a subscription? No. Luna Keep has no ads and no subscription. Every feature in version 1 is yours to use; if you would like to support independent development, there is an optional tip jar in Settings.

Privacy policy

常見問題

App 讀不到「健康」的資料：到「設定 > 健康 > 資料取用權限與裝置 > Luna Keep」打開想分享的項目。Luna Keep 的設定頁也會顯示「健康」權限狀態。

一定要有 Apple Watch 嗎？不用。你可以手動輸入體溫計量到的基礎體溫，也可以手動記錄經期、排卵試紙和黏液。

換新 iPhone 怎麼辦？在 Luna Keep 的設定開啟備份，存到 iCloud 雲端硬碟或「檔案」。在新 iPhone 安裝 Luna Keep 後，用你的密碼還原備份檔。密碼遺失時我們無法幫你找回。

可以用 Luna Keep 避孕嗎？不行。估計僅供參考，低易孕日仍可能懷孕。

沒有 Apple Intelligence 的 iPhone 也能用嗎？可以。所有週期規則、體溫圖表、排卵試紙整理、PDF 匯出與加密備份，在任何執行 iOS 26 的 iPhone 上都能完整運作。無法使用 Apple Intelligence 時，每日說明與週期回顧會改用內建本機文字範本。

拍攝的排卵試紙照片會上傳嗎？不會。試紙照片全程在你的 iPhone 上對齊與處理，僅私密儲存在 App 內（不存入系統相簿，絕不上傳伺服器）。只有在你主動開啟密碼加密備份時，才會一併加密備份。

需要付費訂閱嗎？不需要。Luna Keep 沒有廣告也沒有訂閱，第 1 版的所有功能都可以直接使用；如果你想支持獨立開發，可以在設定中贊助。
