Luna Keep Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Luna Keep does not collect, store or share your data on any server. Everything you log and everything the app works out stays on your iPhone.

What the app uses, and where it stays

  • Apple Health. With your permission, Luna Keep reads cycle data, ovulation tests, cervical mucus, basal and wrist temperature, heart rate, sleep, symptoms and your date of birth from Health, and saves the entries you log back to Health. You choose each type in the Health permission screen and can change it at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices. Health data is never used for advertising or sold.
  • On-device storage. Data that Health cannot hold (for example notes, settings and ovulation test photos) is stored in the app on your iPhone, protected by iOS file protection and excluded from iCloud and computer backups.
  • On-device AI. On iPhones where Apple Intelligence is available, daily summaries and cycle reviews are rewritten by Apple's on-device language model; otherwise, built-in on-device text templates are used. Nothing you log is sent off the device.
  • Personal summary PDFs. If you choose to create a PDF summary of your logged history, it is generated in memory on your iPhone and handed directly to iOS Files. Luna Keep keeps no copy and sends nothing over the network.
  • App lock. If you turn on App Lock, Face ID or passcode verification is handled entirely on-device by iOS. Luna Keep never receives or stores biometric data.
  • Backups you make. If you turn on backups, the app writes a file encrypted with your passphrase to a folder you pick, such as a folder on this iPhone or in your iCloud Drive. We cannot open these files or see your passphrase.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the device. No notification passes through our servers.
  • Camera and photos. Photos of ovulation tests are taken or picked only when you choose to, and stay in the app on your iPhone.

What we do not do

  • The app has no account, no sign-in and no server. It makes no network connections of its own.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party software kits.
  • We do not sell, rent or share health data.

Optional in-app tips. If you choose to leave an optional tip in Settings, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details, no health data is involved, and Luna Keep does not store purchase history.

Crash reports. If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They contain technical details about the crash, not your health data. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Your choices. You can see, change or delete your entries in the app and in the Health app. Deleting Luna Keep deletes everything it stored on the iPhone. Entries it saved to Health stay in Health until you delete them there. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give access to, correct or delete on a server.

Children. Luna Keep is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region).

Not a medical device. Luna Keep is a personal cycle-tracking and wellness app for informational purposes only. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any medical condition, is not intended for the control or support of conception, and must not be used for contraception. Always talk to a healthcare professional about your health.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Luna Keep 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-25

Luna Keep 不會在任何伺服器上收集、儲存或分享你的資料。你記錄的內容和 app 計算出來的結果，都只留在你的 iPhone 上。

App 使用哪些資料，存在哪裡

  • Apple「健康」：經你允許後，Luna Keep 會從「健康」讀取經期、排卵試紙、子宮頸黏液、基礎體溫與手腕溫度、心率、睡眠、症狀和你的出生日期，並把你記錄的項目寫回「健康」。每一項都由你在權限畫面選擇，也可以隨時在「設定 > 健康 > 資料取用權限與裝置」修改。健康資料絕不用於廣告，也不會出售。
  • 裝置上的儲存：「健康」無法保存的資料（例如備註、設定和排卵試紙照片）存在你 iPhone 上的 app 裡，受 iOS 檔案保護，且不會進入 iCloud 或電腦備份。
  • 裝置上的 AI：在可使用 Apple Intelligence 的 iPhone 上，每日說明與週期回顧由 Apple 本機語言模型改寫；無法使用時則改用內建的本機文字範本。你的任何記錄都不會因此傳出裝置。
  • 個人週期摘要 PDF：當你選擇建立個人週期紀錄 PDF 時，檔案僅在你的 iPhone 記憶體中即時產生，並直接交由 iOS「檔案」儲存。Luna Keep 不保留副本，也不經由網路傳送。
  • App 鎖定：若你開啟 App 鎖定，Face ID 或密碼驗證完全由 iOS 在裝置上處理，Luna Keep 無法取得也不會儲存任何生物辨識資料。
  • 你建立的備份：開啟備份後，app 會用你的密碼加密一個檔案，存到你選的資料夾，例如這支 iPhone 或你的 iCloud 雲端硬碟。我們無法打開這些檔案，也不知道你的密碼。
  • 通知：提醒在裝置上排程，不經過我們的伺服器。
  • 相機與照片：只有在你選擇時才會拍攝或選取排卵試紙照片，照片留在你 iPhone 上的 app 裡。

我們不做的事

  • 沒有帳號、不用登入、沒有伺服器，app 本身不建立任何網路連線。
  • 沒有分析、廣告或追蹤，也沒有第三方套件。
  • 不出售、出租或分享健康資料。

App 內贊助：若你在設定中選擇贊助，付款完全由 Apple 透過 App Store（StoreKit）處理。我們不會取得你的姓名、Apple ID 或付款資訊，過程不涉及任何健康資料，Luna Keep 也不會儲存你的購買紀錄。

當機報告：如果你在 iOS 設定中選擇與 App 開發者分享分析資料，Apple 可能會傳送匿名的當機報告給我們。內容是當機的技術細節，不含你的健康資料。可以在「設定 > 隱私權與安全性 > 分析與改進」關閉。

你的選擇：你可以在 app 和「健康」裡查看、修改或刪除記錄。刪除 Luna Keep 會刪掉它存在 iPhone 上的所有資料；它寫入「健康」的項目會留在「健康」，直到你在那裡刪除。因為我們沒有保存任何關於你的資料，伺服器上沒有可供查閱、更正或刪除的內容。

兒童：Luna Keep 不是為 13 歲以下（或你所在地區法定最低年齡以下）兒童設計的。

非醫療器材：Luna Keep 僅為個人月經週期與健康紀錄工具，內容僅供一般參考，不能診斷、治療或監測任何疾病，非用於控制或輔助受孕，也不可用於避孕。身體狀況請諮詢醫療專業人員。

政策變更：如有變更，我們會更新本頁和生效日期。
