Luna Keep

Log and understand your menstrual cycle, temperature trends and LH strip photos—privately on your iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone · iOS 26+ · English & 繁體中文

Clear temperature charts

Brings together Apple Watch sleeping wrist temperature or basal thermometer readings, drawing a cover line with published 3-over-6 temperature shift rules and highlighting sustained shifts.

On-device LH strip log

Snap or import photos of your LH test strips with an alignment guide, view a visual T/C reference ratio and keep a tidy strip timeline—photos never leave your iPhone.

Plain-language summaries

Fixed rules compute every cycle day and estimate. On supported iPhones, on-device Apple Intelligence rewrites those facts into plain daily summaries—and when Apple Intelligence isn't available, built-in on-device templates take over.

Personal cycle record PDF

Export a neat PDF of your own logged cycle history for your records or to bring to an appointment. Intimacy logs are optional and off by default, and Luna Keep keeps no copy.

Apple Health & encrypted backups

Entries you log save back to Apple Health so your history stays yours. Optional passphrase-encrypted automatic backups to your own iCloud Drive or Files make switching iPhones simple—unreadable even to us.

Private by design, no subscriptions

No account, no servers, no ads and no tracking. Every feature is yours to use without a subscription, with an optional tip jar in Settings to support independent development.

Luna Keep is a personal cycle-tracking and wellness app for general information only. It is not a medical device, is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, or the control or support of conception, and must not be used for contraception. Pregnancy is still possible on low days.

在你的 iPhone 上私密記錄並了解你的月經週期、體溫變化與排卵試紙照片——免帳號、無伺服器。

即將在 App Store 上架 · 支援 iPhone（iOS 26 以上）

清楚的體溫曲線與覆蓋線

整合 Apple Watch 夜間手腕溫度或基礎體溫計紀錄，依據公開的 3-over-6 體溫規則繪製覆蓋線並標示持續升溫變化。

本機整理排卵試紙照片

內建對齊輔助線方便拍攝或匯入 LH 排卵試紙，直接在本機顯示視覺 T/C 參考比值與時間軸，照片絕不上傳。

固定規則把關的白話整理

所有週期天數與估算皆由固定規則計算；在支援 Apple Intelligence 的 iPhone 上由本機 AI 改寫為白話每日說明，無法使用 Apple Intelligence 時則改用內建本機範本。

一鍵匯出個人週期紀錄 PDF

將自己記錄的週期歷史整理成乾淨的 PDF，方便自行保存或就診時帶去參考；親密紀錄預設關閉，且 App 不保留副本。

Apple「健康」為核心，加密備份好換機

你記錄的項目會寫回 Apple「健康」，讓紀錄始終屬於你；可開啟個人密碼加密的自動備份至自己的 iCloud 雲端硬碟或「檔案」，換機輕鬆且連開發者都解不開。

無帳號、無廣告、免訂閱

免註冊、無伺服器、無廣告、無追蹤。所有功能皆可直接使用，不需訂閱；若想支持獨立開發，可在設定中贊助。

Luna Keep 僅為個人月經週期與健康紀錄工具，內容僅供一般參考，並非醫療器材，不提供醫療診斷、治療，亦非用於控制或輔助受孕，更不可用於避孕。低易孕日仍有懷孕可能。
