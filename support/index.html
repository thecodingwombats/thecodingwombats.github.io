Luna Keep Support

Questions or problems? Email thecodingwombats@gmail.com. Please do not send health data by email.

Common questions

The app shows no data from Health. Open Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Luna Keep and turn on the types you want to share. Luna Keep also shows Health access in its own Settings.

Do I need an Apple Watch? No. You can type basal temperature from a thermometer and log your period, LH tests and mucus by hand.

How do I move to a new iPhone? Turn on backups in Luna Keep's Settings and save to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new iPhone, install Luna Keep and restore the file with your passphrase. We cannot recover a lost passphrase.

Can I use Luna Keep for contraception? No. Its estimates are for information only, and pregnancy is possible on low days.

常見問題

App 讀不到「健康」的資料：到「設定 > 健康 > 資料取用權限與裝置 > Luna Keep」打開想分享的項目。Luna Keep 的設定頁也會顯示「健康」權限狀態。

一定要有 Apple Watch 嗎？不用。你可以手動輸入體溫計量到的基礎體溫，也可以手動記錄經期、排卵試紙和黏液。

換新 iPhone 怎麼辦？在 Luna Keep 的設定開啟備份，存到 iCloud 雲端硬碟或「檔案」。在新 iPhone 安裝 Luna Keep 後，用你的密碼還原備份檔。密碼遺失時我們無法幫你找回。

可以用 Luna Keep 避孕嗎？不行。估計僅供參考，低易孕日仍可能懷孕。
